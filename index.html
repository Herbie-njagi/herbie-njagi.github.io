About Us

Skills for life, work and enterprise

Muraga Technical Training Institute is a technical and vocational education and training (TVET) institution committed to competence-based training that combines theory with hands-on practice, producing skilled, innovative and self-reliant graduates. We operate from two campuses: Main Campus and Kaare Campus.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Be in the know

      How can I apply?

      Use the online application form in the Admissions section, or visit the admissions office at either campus. Learners may also apply through KUCCPS for government-sponsored programmes.

      What courses do you offer?

      Browse each department under Departments & Courses. Use the campus filter to see what is offered at the Main Campus and Kaare Campus.

      Where can I find the fee structure?

      Download it from the Admissions section. For clarifications, contact the campus office.

      Do you have more than one campus?

      Yes. We operate the Main Campus and the Kaare Campus. Contact details for both are listed at the bottom of this page.
